Professional membership

Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence

I have been a member of AAAI since 2025. It is a membership in a professional society, not a research appointment, and I list it here for what it is.

What it involves

Membership gives me access to AAAI seminars and to the society’s publications and discussions. I use it to follow current research on AI ethics, model alignment, and evaluation, and on where AI meets human–computer interaction, and to take part in conversations with researchers from universities and industry.

Why it matters to my work

My books and essays make conceptual arguments about what AI systems do to human judgment. Those arguments are only worth making if they hold up against what researchers are actually finding. Following the field’s work on alignment and evaluation is how I check early ideas against serious technical and philosophical standards before they become chapters.

The other direction matters too. Through Synaperge I could see how non-specialists interpret AI systems—where misunderstandings start and which explanations actually help. Both inform the writing.
