Research & Projects

Research & publications.

Revolutionizing Household Finance: A User-Centered Platform for Holistic Financial Management

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-13164-5_2

In this first-author HCII 2025 study, I examine how fragmented consumer finance ecosystems push people to juggle unstable mental models across budgeting, investing, and payment tools. I build a user-centered systems framework through comparative interface analysis and propose integrated design principles that support clearer long-horizon financial decisions.

Human-Centered, Sustainable, and Socially Responsible Innovation: Strategic Leadership in the Age of AI and Global Outsourcing

ISBN: 979-8248682874

In this 257-page book, I map what I call the Great Outsourcing: a structural shift where organizations externalize not only execution, but also judgment and institutional memory to automated systems and distributed partners. I offer a decision architecture that helps leaders align productivity, social responsibility, and strategic resilience under accelerated technological change.

Echoes in the Machine: Information Warfare and Reality Collapse in the Post-Truth Age

ISBN: 979-8296185310

In this research monograph, I investigate how algorithmic amplification, generative content loops, and adversarial narrative engineering can destabilize the shared epistemic baseline of digital publics. I introduce relevance gravity and synthetic consensus as analytical tools to study governance risk, civic trust, and information integrity.

AAAI Research Activity: Ethics, Alignment, and Human-Centered Evaluation in Contemporary AI Systems

My membership at the Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AAAI) supports my broader research and practice by helping me stay engaged with frontier debates in safety, alignment, and evaluation, then translate those insights into grounded criteria for designing and assessing human-centered AI in high-stakes settings.
