Revolutionizing Household Finance: A User-Centered Platform for Holistic Financial Management

Henglong Dang, Fang Ding · In M. Schrepp & M. Rauterberg (eds.), HCI International 2025 – Late Breaking Papers, Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol. 16342, pp. 22–40. Springer, Cham.

A mixed-methods study (survey N = 342; interviews N = 35) of the cognitive burden created by fragmented household finance tools, and an integrated prototype with shared family planning, evaluated through iterative usability testing. Presented at the 27th International Conference on Human–Computer Interaction.

02Books

Books

Conceptual arguments, not empirical studies. Both are about what algorithmic systems do to human judgment.

2026 · 257 pages

Human-Centered, Sustainable, and Socially-Responsible Innovation: Agency, Judgment, and Design Choices in Algorithmic Systems

Introduces the “Great Outsourcing”—the handing of judgment, not just tasks, to automated systems—and traces how suggestions, defaults, and rankings turn assistance into authority. Argues for systems designed to invite dissent.

ISBN 979-8248682874Amazon

2025 · 175 pages

Echoes in the Machine: Relevance Gravity of Generative AI

Develops a vocabulary for the cognitive effects of generative AI, starting from relevance gravity—the pull of ranking systems toward what is most engaging—and following it through learning, language, ethics, and authority.

ISBN 979-8296185310Independently publishedAmazon
